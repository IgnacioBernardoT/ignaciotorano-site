Free in Tampa · Football Season

Score a Free
Busch Gardens
Ticket

When the Tampa Bay Buccaneers reach 10 first downs in a regular-season game, fans can register for a free Busch Gardens Tampa Bay single-day ticket from Kane's Furniture.

Check Official Registration

No purchase necessary · 18+ · While supplies last

How It Works

Three Simple Steps

1

Bucs Get 10 First Downs

When the Bucs get 10 or more first downs in a regular-season game, registration opens for the promotion.

2

Register Online

Complete the form at bucs.kanesfurniture.com after a qualifying game. You must be 18+ and use the name on your driver's license.

3

Redeem In Person

Bring your online voucher and valid driver's license to any Kane's Furniture showroom within 3 days of the qualifying game.

Important Details

Good to Know

Must Be 18 or Older

You must be 18 years of age or older to participate.

Limited Quantities

First-come, first-served. Very limited quantities remain per store.

Valid Driver's License

Present a valid driver's license matching the name on the voucher.

Redeem In Person Only

Bring your voucher to any Kane's showroom. No one else may redeem for you.

Within 3 Days

Redeem within three calendar days following a qualifying game.

While Supplies Last

Registration and receiving a voucher do not guarantee a ticket.

Frequently Asked Questions

FAQ

When can I register for a free ticket?

Registration opens after any Tampa Bay Buccaneers regular-season game in which the Bucs record 10 or more first downs. Register online at bucs.kanesfurniture.com after the qualifying game.

How many tickets can I get?

One Busch Gardens Tampa Bay single-day ticket per household, per regular season, according to the official rules. (The registration form's fine print says one per person — either way, plan on one.)

Where do I redeem my ticket?

In person at any Kane's Furniture showroom, within 3 calendar days of the qualifying game and before the deadline printed on your voucher. No one else can redeem it for you.

Do I need a driver's license?

Yes. You must be 18 or older, and you need a valid driver's license matching the name you registered with. The form asks for your license number.

Do I have to buy anything?

No purchase necessary. By registering you agree to receive promotional emails from Kane's Furniture and Busch Gardens.

Is a ticket guaranteed?

No. Tickets are first-come, first-served while supplies last, and quantities are limited per store. Registering and receiving a voucher do not guarantee a ticket.
