Things to Do in Tampa

Where I actually take my family, my clients, and out-of-town friends — from sunrise on Bayshore to Sunday at Ray Jay. No sponsored listings, just the places I'd send you myself.

A quick word from Ignacio: Choosing a neighborhood is really choosing how you'll spend your weekends. Use this list to test-drive Tampa before you buy — and if you want to know which neighborhoods put these within walking or biking distance, ask me.

Waterfront & Outdoors

Bayshore · Riverwalk · Parks

South Tampa

Bayshore Boulevard

Roughly 4.5 miles of uninterrupted waterfront sidewalk along Hillsborough Bay. Walk it at sunrise once and you'll understand why people move here.

Explore Bayshore

Downtown · Channel District

Tampa Riverwalk

A 2.6-mile path along the Hillsborough River linking parks, museums, Armature Works, and Sparkman Wharf. Rent a bike or just wander.

Official Site

Interbay · South Tampa

Ballast Point Park

Fishing pier, splash pad, and a playground right on the bay. A go-to with my own kids.

Explore Ballast Point

Port Tampa

Picnic Island Park

Beach, boat ramp, dog beach, and some of the best sunsets in the city — South Tampa's best-kept secret.

Read My Port Tampa Guide

Davis Islands

Davis Islands

Bike the loop, watch small planes at Peter O. Knight Airport, and let the dog run at the dog beach.

Explore Davis Islands
