Every city has houses with stories. Tampa has pioneer farmhouses, Victorian cottages, Bayshore estates, boom-era Mediterranean villas and modern mansions tied to the people who shaped the city. This series starts with the oldest and moves forward through Tampa history.

I'm a South Tampa native, a dad and a Realtor. I built this map so you can see how the stories connect geographically — then open each house for its address, photos, architecture and history.
