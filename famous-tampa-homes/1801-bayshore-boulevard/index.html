The Estate at 1801 Bayshore Boulevard

A modern Bayshore estate known for its extraordinary scale and its connection to Tampa business leader Don Wallace.

Home › Famous Tampa Homes › 1801 Bayshore Boulevard Estate
Address1801 Bayshore Boulevard, Tampa
BuiltModern era
StyleLuxury estate
NeighborhoodHyde Park / Bayshore
Known ForDon Wallace, founder of Lazydays RV

Not every famous Tampa home is historic in the preservation sense. Some become landmarks because of their scale, their location and the business figures associated with them. The estate at 1801 Bayshore Boulevard is one of the clearest modern examples.

A Modern Bayshore Landmark

Tampa Magazine has described the property as one of the boulevard's most recognizable mansions. The estate sits on more than an acre and a half and contains roughly 15,000 square feet of living space — an exceptional amount of land and house for modern South Tampa.

The Don Wallace Connection

The property was previously owned by Don Wallace, founder of Lazydays RV. Public records and published Tampa real-estate coverage document Wallace's connection to the address. That business-leader history is why the property fits this series even though it is far younger than the houses that came before it.

Why It Matters to Tampa Real Estate

The story here is land. Large Bayshore parcels have become increasingly rare as redevelopment replaces some single-family estates with high-rise residential buildings. An estate of this scale shows just how unusual a large privately held parcel on Bayshore has become.

Did You Know?

One reason 1801 Bayshore stands out is that recreating an estate of similar scale today would require assembling a parcel that is increasingly difficult to find along the boulevard.

"This is the modern end of the same Bayshore story: location, land and scarcity. The architecture changed, but the address never stopped mattering." — Ignacio Toraño
